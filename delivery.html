Service delivery policy

Effective November 22, 2026. Fulfilment of the digital services described on this website is electronic.

1. What we deliver

We provide digital work such as website files, deployed websites, configuration documentation, and SEO or analytics reports as defined in the accepted quotation. We do not ship physical products under these digital service packages.

2. Typical package targets

  • Starter business website — target 5 business days
  • E-commerce website — target 10 business days
  • Monthly SEO and performance — reporting and milestones according to the agreed monthly scope

These are the indicative targets from our prior site, not automatic commitments. The written quotation specifies when timing begins, what client materials are needed and how milestone dates may change.

3. Electronic handover

Depending on scope, deliverables may be shared through an agreed secure file transfer, project repository, staging or production deployment, and email. Credentials must be transferred securely, not pasted into publicly accessible pages.

4. Review and acceptance

Where required, a staging preview will be provided for client testing before launch. The number of revisions and acceptance process will be documented in the quotation.

5. Delays and support

If you have not received a scheduled deliverable, email sales@ransangroups.com with your service reference. We will investigate and advise next steps.
