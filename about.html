A services-focused MSME with multiple registered units. This website describes technology and digital marketing services in particular.

Who we are

RanSan Groups is listed as a proprietary microenterprise under Udyam. The business certificate identifies services as its major activity, and records a registered office in Porur, Chennai.

RanSan Technology and RanSan Digital Marketing are two of its registered units. We present them clearly on this website to help customers understand which services they can enquire about.
