Frequently asked questions

Information to help you understand how we work before starting a project.

Who will issue my service invoice?

For the digital services described on this website, the intended legal enterprise is RanSan Groups. The invoice and recipient bank details must match its verified business and proprietor records. Confirm the final billing name in your signed quotation.

Can I make a payment on this site?

No. This is a static information and enquiry website, not a live payment checkout. Any payment arrangements will be shared only through agreed, authorised channels and written invoices.

Are hosting subscriptions included in the project prices?

Not automatically. Third-party hosting and domain fees should be explicitly itemised in the quotation when applicable.

Are website delivery dates guaranteed?

Published package dates are targets after written acceptance and receipt of required client inputs. Your final milestones and acceptance criteria are in your confirmed quotation or statement of work.

Do you receive a commission for hosting recommendations?

No affiliate tracking links are currently active on this website. If approved affiliate links are added, they will be marked and disclosed.

What happens if I cancel a project?

Please read our cancellation and refund policy before ordering. The amount refundable depends on work completed, non-refundable third-party purchases, and applicable legal rights.
