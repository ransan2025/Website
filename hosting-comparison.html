How to compare web hosting plans

A checklist for selecting hosting based on actual website needs rather than promotional claims.

Practical guide · RanSan Technology · Updated November 2026

1. Start with what your site needs

A brochure site, a WordPress blog, and a large online shop require different resources. Estimate traffic, storage, number of websites, expected email accounts, server-side technologies, and future maintenance needs. Avoid paying for capacity you cannot use, but plan an upgrade path.

2. Compare the complete contract cost

Note the introductory term and renewal price separately. Check whether GST, domain registration, email, backups and migrations cost extra. Compare the total cost over the period you plan to use the service, not only the headline monthly equivalent.

3. Check operational features

  • HTTPS certificates for all needed domains
  • Backup frequency, retention, restoration and any fees
  • Data-centre location, CDN availability and server-side caching
  • PHP / database versions or Node.js support if required
  • Support availability and restoration responsibility
  • Cancellation, renewal and upgrade terms

4. Test support and performance

Read the actual plan documentation and service terms. Once hosted, measure performance using realistic pages and traffic. A data-centre location close to customers can reduce latency, but site quality and caching matter as well.

5. Avoid common misunderstandings

NVMe storage, an advertised uptime target, or a CDN does not by itself guarantee fast performance. A managed service reduces some administration but does not remove your responsibility for content, user access, updates or backup testing.

Short selection checklist

  • Expected traffic and software stack documented
  • Introductory and renewal costs compared
  • Backup restoration process reviewed
  • HTTPS and email requirements confirmed
  • Clear owner for updates, monitoring and security
