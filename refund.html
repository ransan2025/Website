Cancellation & refund policy

Effective November 22, 2026. The agreed project quotation provides the definitive commercial terms, subject to applicable law.

1. How to request cancellation

Write to sales@ransangroups.com with your name, project scope, invoice number if one exists, and reason for cancellation. We will acknowledge the request and review work completed, committed third-party purchases and agreed milestones.

2. Before work begins

Where no services have begun and no non-recoverable third-party costs have been incurred, eligible prepaid service fees will ordinarily be refundable in full. Your agreed quotation and applicable law govern the outcome.

3. After work begins

Any refund is determined by the unperformed part of the agreed scope, payments received, completed work and applicable law. We provide an explanation of any deductions. Third-party domain registrations or hosting purchases may be non-refundable where the third-party provider does not permit reversal; these will be identified in the quotation.

4. Monthly work

For any monthly SEO or maintenance service, cancellation stops future work and future billing in line with the agreed billing terms. Refunds for a partially used period are determined by the written agreement, work delivered and applicable law.

5. Service failure or non-delivery

If we cannot deliver an agreed service, contact us to request remediation or an appropriate refund for undelivered work, subject to the contract and applicable law.

6. Refund processing

Once the refund amount and method are confirmed, we will communicate the expected processing time in writing. Actual credit time depends on the bank or payment method. We do not currently operate a live online payment gateway on this website.

7. Support

Email sales@ransangroups.com or call +91 81486 10567 for assistance.
